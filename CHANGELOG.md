# Changelog

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-25

- **Waylo is now RingHawk**, to match the rest of the Bytehawk "Hawk" apps.
- New icon: a phone handset with ring waves.
- The app is now `RingHawk.exe` and keeps its settings in `%AppData%\RingHawk\`.
- Installing 0.1.1 over Waylo 0.1.0 upgrades it in place and removes the old Waylo shortcuts.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-21

First preview for Windows (released as **Waylo**): the app's shell.

- Hawkline sidebar (navy, like Guardrail EHS and WillowWallet) with Chats, Calls, Contacts, Meetings and Voicemail.
- Chats preview built from the RingHawk mockup, with made-up sample people and messages: the chat list with
  available / away / busy / offline dots, an open conversation with a "video call ended" line, a shared file
  and a typing indicator, voice and video call buttons, and the message box.
- Calls, Contacts, Meetings and Voicemail are "coming soon" pages that describe what they will do and list
  their planned features.
- Settings: light, dark or match Windows. Account, audio and video, notifications and sounds, and privacy are
  marked coming soon.
- The Waylo icon: a speech bubble with a wave-shaped "W".
- Installer and portable zip, installable and updatable from the Bytehawk Studio Launcher.
